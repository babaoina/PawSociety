Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PawSociety

Is PawSociety free to use?

Yes! PawSociety is completely free for all users. No hidden fees, no premium subscriptions.

How do I report a lost pet?

Simply tap the + button, add your pet's photo, details, and location. Your post will be visible to everyone in your area.

Can I adopt through the app?

Absolutely! Browse adoption posts, contact the poster directly through chat, and arrange meetups.

Is my data safe?

We take privacy seriously. Your contact info is only shared when you initiate a conversation.
